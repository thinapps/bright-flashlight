## Full description

Looking for the brightest flashlight app and powerful utility torch in one lightweight tool? Bright Flashlight activates your phone's built-in camera flash to instantly deliver a high-lumen LED light for any dark environment. Easily switch from the default torch mode with adjustable brightness to a reliable emergency light using our built-in SOS mode. You can also tap into the strobe light mode with adjustable speeds, or transform your display into a screen light with either calming or urgent color light swatches in a single tap.

Built for demanding situations, Bright Flashlight features advanced hardware-level integration and custom tool sets. Our adjustable brightness mode automatically detects your device's supported system brightness levels to let you choose the exact light output you need. For signaling and visibility, the integrated strobe light mode features 5 precise speed levels ranging from 1Hz to 5Hz. In critical moments, our emergency SOS mode flashes the universal Morse code distress pattern to attract rescue attention. Additionally, a built-in auto-off timer can be set anywhere from 5 minutes to 1 hour, allowing for safe, hands-free handling or mounting your device out of reach without draining your battery.

Advanced Lighting Modes

* High-Power Torch Mode: Defaults instantly to the maximum brightest flashlight level your hardware can safely deliver. On compatible devices, you can smoothly reduce the light output to your exact preference using an easy slider control.
* Adjustable Strobe Light: Cycle through 5 precise frequency levels from 1Hz to 5Hz using an easy speed slider. Perfect for high-visibility signaling, outdoor safety, or parties.
* Emergency Light & SOS: Activate the built-in SOS mode to flash the universal Morse code distress pattern. Use this emergency light signal to alert search and rescue teams if you are stranded in the outdoors, lost at sea, or in life-threatening situations.
* Screen Light & Color Light: Transform your device display into a full-screen lantern with gentle color light swatches for reading and bedside glows, or choose bold, urgent colors for entertainment and alternative communication needs.

Key Features

* Super bright LED flashlight capability that automatically maxes out your device hardware brightness level
* Zero-fuss torch light utility controls utilizing your phone camera flash with no bloat or unnecessary clutter
* Strobe light mode with an easy speed adjustment slider to cycle through 5 precise frequency levels between 1Hz and 5Hz
* Emergency SOS mode that flashes the universal Morse code distress pattern using high-precision timing
* Screen light with color light preset tray for soft display illumination, alternative communication, or visual entertainment
* Signature auto-off timer options to preserve battery life during hands-free handling with settings from 5 minutes to 1 hour
* Clean interface with no login or internet required that securely remembers your last saved configuration settings
